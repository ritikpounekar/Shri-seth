Cooking for Rau since 1987

Shri Seth Kripa, known across Indore as Guru Kripa, has served pure vegetarian food since 1987. The Rau outlet sits on A.B. Road in Pigdamber, in front of Medicaps University, which makes it an easy stop for students, families and anyone passing through.

Come for the Badkul Shahi Thali, stay for the rooftop. The food is honest, the portions are generous and the bill stays easy on the pocket.

Start with the thali

If it is your first visit, order the Badkul Shahi Thali. It is a full plate meal and the simplest way to try the kitchen.
